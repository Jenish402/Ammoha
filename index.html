About Ammoha Cosmetics

Where nature meets nurture

Beauty is born from purity. We craft makeup and skincare that are as pure as they are powerful.

Our story

We believe beauty is more than skin deep. It is a celebration of self-care, heritage and harmony with nature.

Rooted in ancient traditions and elevated by modern science, we craft skincare and beauty essentials that are as pure as they are powerful. At Ammoha, we blend the wisdom of ancient Indian oil rituals with modern scientific precision to create an experience that goes beyond the surface.

Questions, answered

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before you order.

Are Ammoha products vegan and cruelty-free?

Yes. Every product is 100% plant-based, made without animal-derived ingredients, and never tested on animals.

Do you offer free shipping?

Yes. Shipping is free on every order across India, whatever the order value. There are no hidden handling charges.

How long does delivery take?

Orders are dispatched within 1 to 2 business days and usually arrive 3 to 7 business days after dispatch. Remote areas can take a little longer. You get a tracking number by SMS or email once your order ships.

Which payment methods can I use?

You can pay by credit or debit card, UPI or net banking, and Cash on Delivery is available where offered.

How do I use a coupon code?

Enter your code at checkout and the discount is applied to eligible products instantly. Try SAVE10 for 10% off.

Can I return a product?

You can request a return or replacement within 15 days of delivery if the item is defective, damaged, wrong or missing. It must be unused and in its original packaging. See the Return and refund policy for the steps.

Are the products suitable for all skin tones and types?

Our formulas are made to be inclusive and gentle for every skin tone and texture. If you have sensitive skin, patch test a new product first.

Before you buy

Product FAQs

Quick answers about this product, delivery and returns.

Is this product vegan and cruelty-free?

Yes. Like everything at Ammoha it is plant-based, free of animal-derived ingredients and never tested on animals.

How do I choose the right shade?

Pick a swatch above to see its photos. If you are between two shades, the Shade Finder and our blog guides on skin tones can help, or message us through the Contact page.

How long will my order take to arrive?

Dispatch takes 1 to 2 business days and delivery takes 3 to 7 business days after that. Shipping is free across India.

What if the product arrives damaged or wrong?

Raise a return from My Orders within 15 days of delivery with a clear photo. Our team replies within 48 hours. The item should be unused and in its original packaging.

Can I pay on delivery?

Cash on Delivery is available where offered. You can also pay by card, UPI or net banking.

Need a quick answer?

Common questions

You may find your answer here before writing to us.

How quickly will you reply?

We aim to reply to every message within 48 hours.

Where is my order?

You receive a tracking number by SMS or email once your order is dispatched, usually within 1 to 2 business days. Delivery takes 3 to 7 business days after that.

How do I start a return or replacement?

Open My Orders, select the order, choose Request Return and attach photos if the item is damaged, wrong or defective. Requests must be raised within 15 days of delivery.

Do you ship outside India?

We deliver across India. For international shipping, email contact@ammoha.com.

How can I reach you?

Call 95107 94890, email ammohacosmetics@gmail.com or use the form on this page.
